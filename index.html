      03 point whole files, left where they live

      Files stay put. Pointers travel.

      • pointa file at its source: size, chunk table, blake3 per chunk, one Merkle root
      • namethe pointer note by its own blake3, 26 characters
      • readonly the chunk you need, by HTTP Range, from the source
      • checkblake3(chunk) against its row; the root binds every row

        check one pointer, live

        A Sentinel-2 near-infrared band over Bengaluru

        The whole band is a cloud-optimised GeoTIFF on AWS Open Data. The pointer is a note on emem.dev that names it by chunk hashes. Your browser checks the note, the root and one chunk, reading the chunk from AWS, not from us.
