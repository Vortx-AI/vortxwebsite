eudr.dev

A check on whether a supply plot is deforestation-free under the EU Deforestation Regulation, built from public satellite records — and backed by a receipt you can hand to an auditor.

What it does

The EUDR asks companies to show that commodities like coffee, cocoa, soy and timber weren't grown on recently deforested land. eudr.dev takes a plot boundary and answers that question from the open forest-change record in emem — so the answer comes with a signed source, not just a score. It's the same idea as the rest of Vortx: a fact you can check, applied to a question a lot of businesses now have to answer.

It's in testing with early users. If your sourcing or compliance team needs this, tell us about your case and we'll bring you in as it's ready.
